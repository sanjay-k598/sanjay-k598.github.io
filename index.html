About

I’m a passionate Cloud & DevOps Engineer who loves turning ideas into scalable, secure, and automated systems. My curiosity for how things work drives me to explore infrastructure design, CI/CD automation, and container orchestration — building solutions that are both reliable and elegant in execution.

Beyond cloud pipelines and Terraform scripts, I find creativity in the kitchen 🍳. Cooking helps me unwind and reminds me that great systems - just like great meals need the right ingredients, timing, and balance. Always eager to learn, share, and refine - whether it’s in code, cloud, or cuisine.
